# divvy × crypto twitter — the pivot memo (researched 2026-07-07)

Two research sweeps behind this: (1) how consumer crypto apps won young
crypto-native men 2024–26 (Phantom, pump.fun, Polymarket, Jupiter, friend.tech,
Blur/Blast, Moonshot, Vector, DeBank, Zora, $DEGEN, Blackbird — 27 sources);
(2) CT meme culture, vocabulary, tone, and frog-mascot dynamics (~19 sources).
Full source URLs live in the research transcripts; key ones inline.

## 0. Why this pivot is actually right (not just vibes)

- **Zero onramp friction.** Crypto-native users already hold USDC in a wallet.
  The Stripe/Coinbase/MoonPay onramp applications that block mainstream users
  don't matter here — this is the only audience that can use divvy end-to-end
  TODAY.
- **The lifestyle is the use case.** Hacker houses (mtnDAO: ~175 devs sharing
  a city for a month), conference weeks (Token2049: 20k attendees, 500+ side
  events, shared Airbnbs), co-living cohorts (IslandDAO) — recurring
  multi-person shared expenses among people who are often from four different
  countries, where Venmo/Splitwise settlement literally doesn't work.
- **Stablecoin P2P is mainstream in this demo**: 25–34 men are the heaviest
  stablecoin users; 27% of holders use them for everyday payments; Circle and
  Coinbase both market "split a dinner bill" as a canonical USDC use case.
- **The killer observation** (this writes our content for a year):
  *"you'll ape $500 into a coin named after a hat but won't send Jake $38 for
  the airbnb."*
- Honest caveat: bill-splitting is low-frequency vs. trading apps, so CT
  attention won't self-sustain — the group-chat utility loop has to carry
  retention. And no "onchain Splitwise" has ever broken out (several hackathon
  attempts, none cracked distribution) — the need is real, the distribution is
  the hard part.

## 1. Positioning

**"The group-chat money app for people who already live onchain."**
Degen wrapper, utility core, international-first.

- Lead with culture ("stop settling degen dinners over Zelle like it's 2019"),
  close with utility (non-custodial, settles in seconds, $0.0004 fees, works
  when your roommates are from four countries).
- Flaunt the crypto to this audience (the exact inverse of our normie kit,
  which hides it): USDC, non-custodial, onchain receipts, OPOS ("only possible

- **Loudly, cheekily: NO TOKEN.** Pinned post energy: "mochi is a frog, not a
  coin. there is no token. stop asking." This is (a) a respected
  differentiator post-friend.tech, (b) legal hygiene (SEC treats prospective
  token rewards as the dangerous kind), (c) App Store 3.1.5 hygiene.

## 2. Channel plan (zero budget, ranked by expected payoff)

1. **Founder X account — build-in-public + deadpan shitposting.** The #1
   proven channel. Reply-guy daily (5–20 replies) under Phantom / Jupiter /
   Solana / pump.fun / conference chatter — replies are worth ~27x likes to
   the algorithm. Never reply with a product link.
2. **Solana ecosystem programs** — see §3. The only channel where "new Solana
   consumer app" is itself the content.
3. **Telegram group chats — highest direct conversion.** Crypto bros organize
   trips and dinners IN telegram groups. divvy dropped into 20 real group
   chats beats 1M impressions.
4. **IRL beachhead: one co-living cohort** (mtnDAO / IslandDAO) or Breakpoint
   side-event season. Make divvy the official way one hacker house splits
   groceries for a month → 100 builders → 100 group chats.
5. **Farcaster mini app** (later): "settle this dinner" in-feed; small
   audience (~100k funded wallets) but perfectly native, forgiving of indie
   apps.
6. **Micro-KOL seeding** only when budget exists; meme accounts > talking-head
   influencers for this demo (Polymarket evidence). Zero-budget substitute:
   early access + custom Mochi PFPs.
7. TikTok/IG crypto: lowest priority — that's the audience we just pivoted
   from. (Keep the tiktok-kit; it's the mainstream play for later.)

## 4. Voice guide (the fluency layer)

**One line: talk like the funniest guy in a Solana hacker-house group chat —
lowercase, deadpan, trenches-literate, zero rockets.**

Vocabulary that reads native in 2026: the trenches · cooked / let him cook ·
PvP/PvE (divvy is literally PvE among friends) · bags · exit liquidity ("stop
being jake's exit liquidity") · send it · ape · rug/rugged ("my friend rugged
the dinner bill") · stimmy · anon · ser (always ironic) · degen · alpha ·
based · lock in · down bad · mid-curve/midwit · touch grass · chewing glass
(Solana-builder signal) · OPOS · gm (as reply ritual, not broadcast).

Dated — use only as an ironic bit: wagmi/ngmi, diamond/paper hands, few
understand, probably nothing. **Banned sincerely:** wen lambo, to the moon,
🚀🌕💎🙌, HODL-as-lifestyle, "fam", hashtags, "🔥 BIG NEWS 🔥",
engagement-bait questions, anything that smells AI-generated.

Emoji: 🫡 💀 🤝 🧢 🐸 (ours by right); 🔥 sparingly. Never 😂 (millennial-coded).
Zero emoji is always safer than one wrong emoji.

Register split (Phantom's trick): lowercase memes for everything; proper
capitalization ONLY for security/incident comms — the register shift itself
signals "this one's serious."

Ten rules distilled: write like a person in the group chat, not its sponsor ·
default lowercase · reply-guy daily, never spray · nothing AI-smelling ·
sincere product, ironic delivery · build proper nouns and let them emerge
from replies (settling instantly = getting "mochi'd") · CT emoji only · no
hashtags/giveaway-speak · ship memes on live moments (conference weeks, red
candles: "who's covering dinner after today's candle") · never meme about
users' losses from above, never cosplay as a trading app. Our lane is the
SOCIAL layer of degen life: the group chat, the trip, the dinner, the
conference. Own it completely and we never get caught larping.

## 5. Mochi on CT (frog strategy)

Frogs are CT's national animal — but two landmines:

1. **Never Pepe.** No Pepe-green, no Pepe face, no recognizable Pepe templates
   (sad frog / smug frog / "feels good man"). Furie DMCAs, plus hate-symbol
   baggage. Mochi's mint + distinct art style is the legal and cultural moat.
2. **Never memecoin-signal.** No "$MOCHI", no "something's coming", no
   contract-address jokes. Anchor Mochi to the product in every appearance
   (holding the receipt, tapping the request, 🫡 when the tab settles).

Playbook to copy: FWOG/Jupiter-cats — character warmth + lore + catchphrase,
not a static logo. Mochi's fixed trait: *the frog who always settles up
instantly and is quietly judging your group chat.* Give him one deadpan
signature reply and use it everywhere. Cute art + dry caption = current CT
humor. Cute art + cute caption = normie app.

## 8. Caution flags (hard rules)

- **No points, no airdrop, no token — ever promised.** Airdrop retention data
  is dismal (Uniswap ~7%); friend.tech died of incentive-as-product; SEC
  treats prospective rewards as the dangerous kind. If we ever reward, it's
  retroactive, unannounced, and legal-reviewed first.
- **Apple 3.1.5**: no tokens/crypto as rewards for tasks, downloads,
  referrals, social posts. The Solana dApp Store is the pressure valve.
- **Compliance unchanged**: P2P stays peer-to-peer and non-custodial; nothing
  custodial-looking without counsel; COLLECTOR_WALLET stays vestigial.
- **CT smells manufactured hype**: no undisclosed paid shills, no giveaway
  followers. Founder authenticity is the moat.
- **The app keeps its journal aesthetic.** This is a marketing/channel pivot,
  not a product rebrand — cute product + dry CT voice is the FWOG formula.
- The normie tiktok-kit stays in the repo: it's the mainstream expansion play
  once onramps exist. Don't delete the future.
