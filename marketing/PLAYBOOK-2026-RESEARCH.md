## Verdict on unbranded tips-carousels: REAL, STILL WORKS — but only the original kind
- [STUDY] Carousels win engagement/saves/search durability (+81% ER vs video,
  Fanpage Karma 698K posts); video wins cold reach & shares (~5x reach, Metricool
  2.31M posts). Carousel = conversion + search asset, video = reach engine.
- TikTok OCR-indexes overlay text into search (Keyword Insights). Posts earn views
  for months. Swipes ≈ watch time.
- [OFFICIAL] TikTok actively demotes "unoriginal" static-image templates (enforcement
  escalated Sept 2025). Mass AI slideshows are the thing being suppressed —
  hand-designed original carousels stay fully FYP/search eligible. Divvy's notebook
  aesthetic is an originality moat, not just brand.
- [CASE] Cal AI's engine was actually 250+ creator affiliate network (unique promo
  code per creator, commission on verified subs), not just organic slideshows.
  Locket: nano-influencer seeding + same 2-3s template daily.

## The carousel recipe
6-8 slides. Slide 1: bold keyword-bearing hook, 10-15 words (outcome / number /
contrarian / problem-POV). Slides 2-6: ONE genuinely useful tip each, no branding.
Final slide: earned soft reveal ("the app that does this automatically") + screenshot.
Trending photo-mode audio; caption 200+ chars leading with the exact search phrase;
3-5 keyword hashtags; seed/pin a "what app is this?" comment; link in bio.

## Divvy content mix
- ~50% value carousels (5-7/wk), journal-style, soft Mochi corner-doodle on ~1 in 3
- ~25% skits/POV video (2-3/wk) — roommate/Venmo drama; this is the cold-reach layer
- ~25% Mochi character (2/wk) — brand glue, NOT a standalone reach strategy (no
  verified case of a new small app growing primarily on mascot content; Duolingo
  took years; Finch grew on ads)

## Cadence + accounts
Warm account 3-7 days (human scrolling, first posts non-promo) [PRACT]. Then 1-2/day;
min 4-5 carousels/wk. Follower count is NOT a ranking factor [OFFICIAL] — small
accounts rank in search. NO multi-account farms (dedupe + strikes). One @divvy main +
founder personal build-in-public. IG: 3-5 Reels/wk; sends-per-reach is the metric;
Trial Reels at 1K followers; carousels up to 20 slides + music = Reels-feed eligible;
NEVER post TikTok-watermarked video (recommendation-ineligible) — export clean.

## Landmines
Organic own-account content is NOT under fintech ad rules, BUT any gifted/paid
creator post = Branded Content: toggle required, 18+ audience, origin market, FTC
#ad disclosure. Stay in etiquette/organization framing — never financial advice,
earnings, or "get paid" framing. No referral links in comments (spam-filtered) —
bio only. NO crypto/USDC reward promises anywhere (also App Store rule 3.1.5(v)).

## $0-500 creator seeding
1) Gift lifetime pro to 50-100 nano creators (1K-10K, college/roommate/budget niche)
   via short specific DMs, follow-ups day 3 + 7. 2) Pay 3-5 nanos $20-100/post.
3) Cal AI-style tracking: unique promo code per creator + commission on verified
   subscriptions (not installs); App Store Offer Codes as audience incentive.
4) Campus ambassadors paid in pro access + commission (Frich model). 5) Hold $100-200
   to Spark-boost a proven winner post-launch.

## Honesty notes

Blitzit/Quittr/Rize numbers are founder-circle chatter without primary sources.
