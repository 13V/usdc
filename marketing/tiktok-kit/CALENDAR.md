Rules of thumb: post 6–10pm your audience's time; same content to TikTok +
Reels (carousels → IG carousel + TikTok photo mode); weeks 1–2 are all
ZERO-effort so the habit costs nothing from a beach chair. Concept numbers
reference `CONCEPTS.md`. **Roughly half the slots are value carousels** — the
highest save/share format for app growth right now; the rest is Mochi/chat
comedy so the account has a face, not just tips. Chat dramas are silent
videos — post the `.webm`/`.mp4` to TikTok with a trending sound added in-app,
and use the matching still as slide 1 of an IG carousel.
