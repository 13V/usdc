# Reply-guy kit (copy/paste answers to the usual questions)

**"is it custodial?"**
> nope — non-custodial. it's your wallet, your keys, your money. divvy never holds
> your funds, it just routes the split. cash out to your bank whenever you want.

**"what chain / why solana?"**
> solana. picked it for the ~1s settle and ~$0.001 fees. splitting a $40 dinner
> shouldn't cost more than the tip. it's usdc under the hood, so $1 = $1.

**"what are the fees?"**
> network fee is ~$0.001. that's the whole story — no 3% card skim, no "instant
> transfer" surcharge. dollars in, same dollars out.

**"is there an ios app?"**
> the web app runs great on iphone today — app.divvysol.com, add to home screen and
> it feels native. a real ios build is in testflight beta; reply and i'll get you
> an invite 🐸

**"wen token / is there an airdrop?"**
> the app is the app — split bills, settle up, that's it. no token in the product,
> nothing to buy to use it. community stuff lives out here on X 🐸
